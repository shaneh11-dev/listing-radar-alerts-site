Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Listing Radar Alerts (“Listing Radar Alerts,” “we,” “us”) provides automated listing alerts for classic and collector cars through listingradaralerts.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect only what you give us when you request alerts or contact us:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, and, if you choose text alerts, your mobile phone number.
  • Alert preferences: the vehicles you want tracked and any filters you choose, such as year, transmission, colors, mileage, location, and alert frequency.
  • Text message consent records: if you opt in to text alerts, the date and time of your consent and the consent wording you agreed to.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested.

How we use your information

  • To review your request, confirm details and pricing with you, and set up your alerts.
  • To send the email and, if you opted in, text message alerts you requested, along with service messages about your account.
  • To respond to your questions and provide support.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and keep records of consent.

Text messaging (SMS) information

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or text message opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Your phone number and SMS consent are used only to send you the text alerts you requested and related service messages. Information may be shared with the service providers described below solely so they can deliver those messages for us.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the service, such as website hosting, email delivery, text message delivery, and workflow automation. They may process your information only to perform services for us.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the service.
  • A successor if the business is sold or transferred, in which case this policy would continue to apply to your information.

Retention

We keep your information for as long as you are an active customer and as needed afterward for legitimate business, legal, and record-keeping purposes, including keeping a record of your text message consent. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see “Your choices”), subject to those requirements.

Your choices

  • Text messages: reply STOP to any text to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
  • Email: you can ask us to stop emailing you or cancel your alerts by writing to support@listingradaralerts.com.
  • Access, correction, deletion: contact us at the same address to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
