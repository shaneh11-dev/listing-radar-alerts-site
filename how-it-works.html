Why request-and-respond

A person checks every setup, not just an algorithm.

Classic and collector cars are messier than a make/model/year dropdown — trims, variants and one-off filters matter. A quick human check up front means your alerts are right from day one.

Your exact car, confirmed

Filters set up correctly

Color, mileage, state, transmission and more — we apply them for you so your inbox only shows cars that actually qualify.

A real person to reach

Questions, changes, or unsubscribing all go through a person at support@listingradaralerts.com — not a ticket queue.

Common Questions

Why isn't this instant self-serve?
Because collector-car searches rarely fit a clean dropdown. A quick manual confirmation means your filters are set up correctly the first time, instead of you fixing a mismatch after the fact.
Where do the listings come from?
We scan across a wide range of auction houses, dealer marketplaces, and enthusiast listing sites — not just one source — so you see a match wherever it shows up.

Can I filter by color, mileage, or state?
Yes. Exterior and interior color (including multiple colors), max mileage, hiding TMU listings, specific states, drive side, transmission, and originality can all be set as hard filters on your alerts.
How do I change my filters, cadence, or unsubscribe?
Just email support@listingradaralerts.com. There's no dashboard to log into — a person will make the change directly.
How fast will I start getting alerts?
Most requests are confirmed and live within a day of you submitting the form.
